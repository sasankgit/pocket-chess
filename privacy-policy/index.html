Privacy Policy

Pocket Chess · Neverlos Games · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Pocket Chess handles information when you use the official version of the Android app distributed by Neverlos Games.

Information handled by the game

Pocket Chess does not ask you to create an account, and Neverlos Games does not operate a game-data server for this app. The current board, move history, and selected difficulty are kept temporarily in the app's memory while you play. The game does not send them to Neverlos Games or save them in a persistent game-history database.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Pocket Chess uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display banner and interstitial ads. The SDK may automatically collect and share information with Google and its advertising partners for ad delivery, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on the SDK and your device settings, this may include:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Ad and app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information about the app and SDK.
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.

Google states that Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. Google handles this information under its own policies and settings. You can manage your Android advertising ID and ad personalization using your device's privacy and ads settings. For details, see Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

Neverlos Games does not maintain a copy of your chess games or a profile about you. Information processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices; see Google's Privacy Policy and account controls for more information.

Downloads from unofficial sources

We recommend installing Pocket Chess from its official Google Play listing. APKs from unofficial sources may be altered or repackaged by third parties, and we cannot verify or support those copies or their privacy and security practices. This notice concerns versions that Neverlos Games did not create or distribute and does not limit any rights or responsibilities that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Children

Pocket Chess is not designed to collect personal information from children. The advertising SDK may process device and advertising information as described above. Parents and guardians can manage advertising settings through Android device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page, with its updated date shown above.

Questions

For privacy questions about the official Pocket Chess app, contact Neverlos Games through the support contact on its Google Play listing.
